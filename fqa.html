Frequently Asked Questions

🔍

What specific design services do you offer?

+

I specialize in Digital Art, Character Design, Manga Style illustrations, and high-end UI/UX designs for web and mobile applications.

Can you handle both design and development?

+

Yes, I provide a complete solution. I can design your interface in Figma and develop it using modern tech stacks like React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS.

Do you provide source files for the projects?

+

Absolutely. Upon completion and final payment, I provide all industry-standard source files (PSD, Figma, or GitHub repository access).

Can you create animations for my website?

+

Yes, I integrate motion graphics and interactive animations to make your digital presence more engaging and dynamic.

Do you offer custom character designs?

+

Yes, I create unique, high-quality characters tailored to your brand’s personality, ranging from pixel art to detailed manga styles.

What is your typical project workflow?

+

My process involves four steps: Discovery/Research, Conceptual Sketching, Development/Refinement, and Final Delivery.

How long does a typical project take?

+

Timeline depends on complexity. A character design usually takes 3-5 days, while a full website can take 2-4 weeks.

What is your revision policy?

+

I offer 3 rounds of major revisions to ensure the final result matches your vision. Minor tweaks are always welcome.

Do I need to provide content for the website?

+

Ideally, yes. However, I can help with placeholders and basic structure if your content is still in progress.

What are your payment terms?

+

I typically require a 50% upfront deposit to secure the slot, with the remaining balance due after final approval.

Which payment methods do you accept?

+

I accept Bank Transfer, PayPal, and major Cryptocurrency wallets for international clients.

Are there any hidden costs?

+

No hidden costs. All pricing is discussed and finalized in the initial proposal before we start the work.

How do we keep in touch during the project?

+

We can communicate via WhatsApp for quick updates, or use Email and Slack for formal documentation.

What is your response time?

+

I usually respond within 2-4 hours during business days. For urgent matters, my WhatsApp is always open.

Can we have a video call?

+

Definitely! I’m happy to hop on a Zoom or Google Meet call to understand your requirements in detail.

Do you offer post-project support?

+

Yes, I provide 1 month of free technical support to ensure everything is running smoothly after launch.
